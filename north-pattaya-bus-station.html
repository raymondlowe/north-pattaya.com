Getting to North Pattaya: Which Bus Station, and How to Get Onward

Coaches from Bangkok and from Suvarnabhumi airport set down at Pattaya North Bus Terminal, on North Pattaya Road near Sukhumvit Road. From there it is 3.2–3.5 km to the sand at Wong Amat, 2–5 minutes to the Dolphin Roundabout, and a songthaew ride — not a walk with luggage — to the beach strip.

One thing is worth settling before you travel: not every coach sold as "Pattaya" uses this terminal.

Roong Reuang Coach runs all four routes below from Pattaya North. Fares are per seat in THB and journey times are about 2 hours on the Bangkok and airport routes, traffic dependent. Fares and departure times checked 18 November 2026.

Those are the departures from Pattaya, which are the ones you need for the journey back to Bangkok. Coaches run the same routes in both directions through the day.

The Don Mueang coach does not stop here

The state operator's Don Mueang airport service — 155 THB, six departures each way a day, 36-seat air-conditioned coaches, about 2 hours 30 minutes — boards at a kerbside stop on Sukhumvit Road between North and Central Pattaya, opposite the Father Ray Foundation (มูลนิธิคุณพ่อเรย์). If you bought that ticket, you are not arriving at Pattaya North Bus Terminal, and the ride to Wong Amat or Naklua starts from a different place on the map.

From the terminal to the water

The terminal sits on the Sukhumvit side of North Pattaya Road, east of the Dolphin Roundabout, so every beach destination is a ride rather than a walk.

A shared songthaew — a baht bus — costs 15–20 THB a trip, distance-dependent, since 10 April 2026. If you are carrying luggage, a metered taxi, a Grab or a Bolt booked at the terminal is the simpler choice, and the coach operator also sells a hotel door-to-door transfer. Ask at the counter, or book it when you buy the coach ticket.

If you are staying at this end of town, see hotels in North Pattaya for the parts of the bay and what each one is like to stay in.

Buying a ticket

Tickets for the Ekkamai, Mo Chit 2 and Suvarnabhumi routes can be bought at rrcticket.com or through the operator's LINE account, @PATTAYABUS, and at the counter in the terminal. Bang Na is counter-only.

For a city coach, be at the pick-up point 15 minutes before departure. For the airport service, the operator asks for 30 minutes at the counter to check in. If a delayed flight makes you miss the airport coach, the counter will move you to the next coach with seats for a 50 THB per-person service fee.

Where the terminal is

Pattaya North Bus Terminal, North Pattaya Road (ถนนพัทยาเหนือ), Na Kluea, Bang Lamung, Chonburi 20150. The map pin is 12.94973, 100.90340.

The building answers to four names — "Pattaya Bus Terminal" and "Pattaya North Bus Terminal" on the operator's own pages, and "North Pattaya Bus Station" and สถานีขนส่งพัทยาเหนือ on maps and in local use. They are all the same place.
