The North Pattaya Bus Station

Most visitors to Pattaya arrive by road, and the northern bus station is where a large share of them are dropped. This guide is being written now: it will cover the station itself, the services that use it, and the short trip from the station to the water.

What this guide will answer

  • Which services use this station, and which use the others in town — so you know which one your ticket is for.
  • Fares and journey times from Bangkok and from Suvarnabhumi airport, with the month they were checked.
  • How to get from the station to Wong Amat, to the Naklua end and to the centre, and what each of those costs.
  • Whether a baht bus, a metered taxi or a ride-hailing app is the sensible choice at the hour you arrive.
  • What is around the station: somewhere to leave a bag, somewhere to eat, and what to do if you arrive after dark.

Why this page matters more than it sounds

Arrival is where a trip is most easily spoiled and where the numbers change most often. Fares and timetables are the facts most likely to be out of date on any guide you read, including this one until it is finished — which is why nothing will be printed here without a checked date beside it.
