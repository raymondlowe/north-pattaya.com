North Pattaya

North Pattaya is the northern end of Pattaya bay: the top of the crescent where Pattaya Beach begins, the west-facing shore of Wong Amat behind the headland, the old fishing quarter of Naklua, and the bus terminal on North Pattaya Road where the coaches from Bangkok and the airport set down.

What "north" means on a map

Pattaya City names only the two ends of Pattaya Beach — the Dusit Resort junction in the north and Pattaya Hill in the south — so the line between north, central and south is a convention rather than an official boundary. This guide uses "north" for everything from the Naklua market at the top of the bay down to Pattaya Klang.

The Dolphin Roundabout is the hinge of it: North Pattaya Road leaves it east towards Sukhumvit Road and the bus terminal, Beach Road and Second Road run south from it along the sand, and Naklua Road runs north towards the market and the pier.

Getting around the north bay

Shared songthaews — the baht buses that run fixed routes with no timetable — charge 15–20 THB a trip, distance-dependent, since the fare rose on 10 April 2026. They run north along Naklua Road past the Dolphin Roundabout, and round the Beach Road and Second Road loop behind Pattaya Beach. None of them runs along the sand at Wong Amat: you ride Naklua Road and walk down a lane.

Walking is the practical way to move between the beach and the streets immediately behind it. Between the bay's parts it is not: Wong Amat to the bus terminal is 3.2–3.5 km, and the terminal to Walking Street is 6–7 km, so plan on a songthaew, a motorbike taxi or a car for those legs.

Where to start

Arriving by road? Begin with the North Pattaya bus station: which terminal your ticket is for, the fares, the timetables, and what the ride to the water costs.

Choosing where to sleep? Read Wong Amat and then hotels in North Pattaya — the beach and the room are one decision at this end of town.

Here for a day rather than a week? Naklua for the market and the seafood, and the beach at North Pattaya for the sand with a road behind it.
