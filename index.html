North Pattaya: the northern end of the bay

North Pattaya is the northern end of Pattaya bay. It is a different place from the strip around Walking Street: the beach curves away, the fishing quarter of Naklua sits at the top of it, and the hotels are built back from a shore that is quieter than the centre's.

This site is a guide to that northern end, and nothing else. It is written as one place with five parts, because the questions people arrive with — which beach, which quarter, which station, which hotel — are answered by a part of the bay rather than by the town as a whole.

The five parts of North Pattaya

  • Wong Amat beach — the northern beach of the bay, and the one the resorts were built around.
  • Naklua — the fishing quarter at the top of the bay: its market, its sois, and what is left of the old town.
  • The beach at North Pattaya — the central stretch of the northern bay, and what it is like to spend a day on it.
  • The North Pattaya bus station — where the buses from Bangkok and the airport arrive, and how to get onward from there.
  • Hotels in North Pattaya — what the different parts of the north bay cost to stay in, and who each one suits.

Why this site is thin today

It is new. The five guides above are being written now, and each one opens with the questions it will answer before it opens with an answer. Where a guide is not finished, the page says so rather than filling the space with something generic.

The rule we hold ourselves to is simple: every fare, price, opening time and distance on these pages is checked against a current source and carries the month it was checked. Nothing is copied from another travel guide and presented as our own knowledge.

Where to start

If you are deciding where to stay in Pattaya and want to know what the north end is actually like, start with Wong Amat and then read the hotels guide: the beach and the price are one decision.

If you are arriving and want to know which station you are being dropped at and how far it is from the water, start with the bus station guide.
