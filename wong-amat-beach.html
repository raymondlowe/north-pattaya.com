Wong Amat Beach

Wong Amat is the quiet swim in Pattaya bay: roughly 1.3–1.5 km of west-facing sand, resorts and condominium towers along the frontage, no road behind it, and the rocky cape at Dusit Thani closing its southern end where Pattaya Beach begins. Come here to swim, not to be entertained.

Getting onto the sand

The beach is public. The resorts own most of the frontage, but not the sand, and the way in is down a lane off Naklua Road — Soi 16 and Soi 18 are the established ones, with other lanes between them. Because no public road runs along the back of the beach, there is no beachfront car park, and parking near the sand is limited.

Songthaews run north along Naklua Road and charge 15–20 THB a trip, distance-dependent, since 10 April 2026. None of them runs along the beach itself: you ride the road and walk down a lane to the sand.

The water

Wong Amat faces west into the Gulf, sheltered by the headland to the north and the cape at Dusit Thani to the south, and it is the bay's swimming beach rather than a working waterfront.

It is rock at the ends rather than sand. In September 2025 the city cleared sharp stones and debris from the shoreline after local fishermen reported a buildup where people walk and wade, and moved the rock that could not wash back into the swimming zone. Pattaya City's marine safety unit covers this shore from the Dusit cape north to Hat Krathing Lai, and treats cuts and marine-animal injuries.

Watch the water line at both ends of the beach, and walk in over sand rather than over the rock.

What is on the beach

Behind the sand it is hotel gardens, condominium walls and the lanes that lead back up to Naklua Road. On the sand there are far fewer loungers and vendors than on Pattaya Beach: a handful of operators sell drinks and seafood at the southern end, and the rest of the frontage is largely open.

There is no promenade to stroll and no strip of bars behind the beach. That is the trade you make for the quiet, and it is why the beach suits people whose hotel has the terrace, the pool and the restaurant.

Where to stay behind it

Rooms behind the beach run from the resort frontage to condominium lets in the lanes off Naklua Road. See what a room behind the beach costs for the parts of the bay and what each one is like.
