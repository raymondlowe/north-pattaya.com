Wong Amat Beach

Wong Amat is the beach at the northern end of Pattaya bay. This guide is being written now: it opens with the questions a visitor arrives with, and it will not open with an answer until that answer has been checked.

What this guide will answer

  • How you reach the sand, and which sois lead down to it from the road behind.
  • What the beach is actually like: the sand, the water, the tide and the rocks the tide exposes.
  • Shade, loungers and what they cost on the day you read this.
  • Swimming, and whether there are flags or water-quality warnings in force.
  • What the beach backs onto, and where to eat within walking distance of it.

How this page will be written

Beaches are the subject most often described from photographs rather than from the sand, so this page is held to a stricter rule than the rest of the site: nothing is described here that has not been seen, and no price appears without the month it was checked.

Until that is done, this page stays short on purpose. A short honest page is worth more to you than a long guessed one.

While you wait

The rest of the north bay is in the same state of preparation: Naklua, the North Pattaya beach, the bus station and the hotels. The North Pattaya hub explains how the five fit together.
