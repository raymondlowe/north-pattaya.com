North Pattaya Beach

The beach at North Pattaya is the central stretch of the northern bay, between Wong Amat and the point where the town's own beachfront begins. This guide is being written now.

What this guide will answer

  • How to reach the sand, and where the access points are from the road behind.
  • What the water and the sand are like, including how the northern bay differs from the beach in the centre of town.
  • Shade, seating and what a lounger costs on the day you read this.
  • Swimming, water sports, and any flags or warnings in force.
  • Where to eat and drink within a few minutes' walk of the sand.

One beach, or two?

This page and the Wong Amat page describe different beaches with different access, different water and different crowds. They are deliberately not merged: a reader choosing where to stay needs to know which stretch they are choosing, and a single page covering both would blur exactly the difference that matters.

The rest of the north bay

Naklua, the bus station and the hotels are being written in the same way. The North Pattaya hub explains how the five fit together.
