North Pattaya Beach

This is a different beach from Wong Amat. The sand here is the northern end of Pattaya Beach — a half-moon crescent about 3 km long that starts at the Dusit Resort junction and runs south to Pattaya Hill — with Beach Road behind it for its whole length, and the town immediately behind the road.

Where the northern stretch runs

Pattaya City names only the two ends of the beach: the Dusit Resort junction in the north and Pattaya Hill at the southern end in South Pattaya. There is no official line dividing north from central, so "the northern stretch" here means the description it sounds like — the sand from the Dolphin Roundabout south to roughly Pattaya Klang.

Behind the sand

Beach Road — ถนนพัทยาสายหนึ่ง — runs the length of the beach and is one-way. Behind it is the dense part of town: hotels, shops, restaurants, bars and cabarets, which is how the city itself describes this seafront.

The northern end is where the hotel stock is thickest on the sand: Dusit Thani Pattaya and A-One Royal Cruise on Beach Road, Grande Centre Point Pattaya behind the roundabout end, and Woodlands Hotel and Resort on the Pattaya–Naklua road side.

Getting there and moving along it

On foot, the pavement runs the length of Beach Road, and the northern end is about a 5-minute walk from the Dolphin Roundabout and Terminal 21.

Songthaews run the Beach Road and Second Road loop for 15–20 THB a trip, distance-dependent, since 10 April 2026 — the same fare as the rest of the bay. From the bus terminal, the northern end of the beach is 1.8–4.2 km depending on the direction of approach, because access to the terminal mouth is one-way: a short songthaew or motorbike-taxi ride rather than a walk with bags.

What it is like

Pattaya City describes the beach the same way along its whole length: trees and shade along the sand, and land and water sports equipment on it.

The northern end is quieter and more hotel-oriented than the middle of the bay, where the retail and nightlife density is, and than the southern end, which finishes at Walking Street. If you want the beach outside your hotel rather than a party street behind it, this is the end to pick.

Choosing between the two beaches

Wong Amat is the quiet one: no road behind the sand, fewer loungers, resorts and condominium towers at your back, and a rockier waterline at the ends. The northern stretch of Pattaya Beach is the convenient one: pavement, songthaews, shops and hotels in a line, and a beach that runs on for another 2 km south of you.

Where to stay on this stretch

Staying on this stretch covers the rooms on and behind Beach Road against the ones behind Wong Amat. For the beach at the other end of the bay, see how it compares with Wong Amat. And if you are coming off a coach, the ride from the station is a few minutes, not a walk. North Pattaya is the wider bay end this stretch belongs to, Wong Amat and Naklua included.
