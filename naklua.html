Naklua

Naklua is the fishing quarter at the northern end of Pattaya bay, and the oldest part of the town. This guide is being written now and will be published here.

What this guide will answer

  • The seafood market: when it runs, what is sold there, and how it compares with buying fish at the beachfront.
  • The sois off Naklua Road — which ones lead to the water, and which are residential.
  • The beach at Naklua: what it is like, and how it differs from Wong Amat next door.
  • What remains of the old fishing village, and where to see it.
  • Where to eat fish in the quarter, at both market and restaurant prices.

Why this page comes before the beach pages

Naklua is the part of the north bay that other guides treat as a footnote, and it is the part where the town's own life is still visible. It is also the part where a visitor can most easily overpay: the seafood trade sits at the market, the prices sit at the beachfront, and the difference between them is the thing worth knowing.

Every figure on the finished page — market days, opening hours, prices — will carry the month it was checked.
