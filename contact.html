Contact north-pattaya.com

For anything to do with this website — a correction, a question about North Pattaya, an advertising or press enquiry — write to contact@wlmedia.com.

Corrections especially welcome

If a fare on this site is out of date, a boat no longer runs, or a price has moved, we would rather hear it from you than keep publishing the old number. Tell us the page and the figure, and if you can, where the correct figure is published. Corrections are made on the page itself, and the checked date beside the figure is updated with them.

Response

This is a small publisher. Corrections are read first and normally answered within a few days. Your email is not added to any list and is not passed to anyone else.
