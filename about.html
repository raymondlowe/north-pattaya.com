About north-pattaya.com

north-pattaya.com is an independent guide to the northern end of Pattaya bay, in Chonburi province, Thailand. It covers five things and only five: the beach at North Pattaya, Wong Amat beach, the fishing quarter of Naklua, the bus station where visitors arrive, and the hotels that line that shore.

What this site is not

It is not a guide to Pattaya as a whole. Walking Street, Jomtien, the Sanctuary of Truth and the town's nightlife are covered by other sites, several of them better established than this one. The northern bay is a small enough subject to be covered properly, and that is the whole of our ambition.

How the facts are handled

The useful facts about this area — bus fares, lounger prices, market hours, hotel rates — change, and they are the facts most often copied from one guide to another without anyone checking them. On this site each one is checked against a current source and published with the month it was checked. Where a figure has not been checked, it does not appear.

The site is new, and several of its guides are still being written. Pages that are unfinished say so at the top rather than being padded out with general advice.

Who publishes it

north-pattaya.com is published by WL Media, which publishes independent travel guides to individual places. Corrections and questions are welcome: see the contact page.
